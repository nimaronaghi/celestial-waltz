Circular-binary verification of the direct Newtonian solver. Two equal masses have total mass M=1, initial separation d0=1, G=1, and softening epsilon=0, in code units. (a) The first particle's orbit at 256 steps per period, compared with the analytic circular solution. (b) Position residual norm, normalized by d0, across 3 periods at the same resolution. (c) Final position residual after 3 periods versus normalized timestep h=Delta t/P. Reference slopes h^2 and h^4 are normalized to 0.4 times the finest measured error for the corresponding method; they are guides, not fits. (d) Absolute relative energy error at 256 steps per period. Exact zeros are omitted from logarithmic axes; positive values are not clipped. These are deterministic numerical errors, not statistical confidence intervals. This experiment verifies a smooth two-body problem and does not establish accuracy for arbitrary many-body systems or galaxy initial conditions.
